Spreadsheet format 
round,category,value,clue,answer,daily_double
J,SCIENCE,200,H2O is the formula for this,What is water?,
J,SCIENCE,400,...,...,yes
DJ,SPACE,400,...,...,
FJ,U.S. STATES,,The only one-syllable state,What is Maine?,

round: J (Jeopardy), DJ (Double Jeopardy) or FJ (Final — one row).
Up to 6 categories × 5 clues per round, in the order you want them on the board.
value is optional (defaults to 200–1000 / 400–2000).
daily_double: put yes on the clues you want. If you mark none, 1 is hidden in J and 2 in DJ at random.
